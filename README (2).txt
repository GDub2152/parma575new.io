Parma 575 Event Component

1. Copy event-component.html into your homepage where you want the event displayed.
2. Merge event-component.css into your site's CSS.
3. Copy events/events.js into your site.
4. Place your flyer image at assets/images/picnic-rsvp-banner.png.
5. Replace PASTE-YOUR-GOOGLE-FORM-LINK-HERE with your Google Forms URL.
6. After the event, change active:true to active:false.
